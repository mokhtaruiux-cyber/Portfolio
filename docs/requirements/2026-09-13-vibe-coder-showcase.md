# Vibe Coder Showcase — Content Brief

## Goal

Expand Mohammed Mokhtar's portfolio positioning beyond product design. The new content should show that he is also a vibe coder and creative engineer who can use AI to turn product thinking into working, demo-ready products.

The story should not imply that AI replaces craft or engineering judgment. It should show that Mohammed combines product strategy, UX, visual design, AI-assisted development, and hands-on implementation to move from an idea to a credible product quickly.

## Core Positioning

Mohammed is a product designer and vibe coder who ships products with AI.

He can:

- Identify and frame a real product problem.
- Design the end-to-end user experience and interface.
- Use AI-assisted workflows to accelerate research, content, prototyping, and development.
- Build connected web and mobile experiences, not just isolated mockups.
- Make practical product and technical decisions while iterating.
- Deliver polished, demo-ready products that stakeholders can see, use, and evaluate.

## Desired Portfolio Narrative

The content should communicate this progression:

1. Mohammed starts with the problem and the people using the product.
2. He defines the product structure, workflows, and interface.
3. He collaborates with AI as an accelerator for exploration, implementation, and iteration.
4. He validates details through direct building and testing.
5. He ships a working product or a credible, demo-ready system rather than stopping at static design files.

## Tone

- Confident, specific, and grounded in evidence.
- Product-led rather than tool-led.
- Modern and ambitious without sounding like hype.
- Clear to founders, hiring managers, product leaders, and engineering partners.
- Avoid generic phrases such as “leveraging cutting-edge AI,” “10x,” or “revolutionary.”
- Avoid claiming production scale, business outcomes, customer adoption, or technical capabilities that are not supported by evidence.

## Deliverables Requested

Create a cohesive content proposal containing:

1. A concise homepage positioning line.
2. A supporting hero paragraph.
3. A short section introducing “Vibe Coding” as a capability.
4. Three to five capability statements that connect product design, AI, and shipping.
5. A showcase introduction that can frame Dawwem, IronCore, RestuHub, LDUN Redesign, and Zain Intranet as proof of the positioning.
6. Improved titles, descriptions, impact statements, and case-study copy for Dawwem and IronCore, plus clearly marked content frameworks for RestuHub, LDUN Redesign, and Zain Intranet where verified details are still required.
7. A strong call to action for founders or teams who need an idea turned into a working product.
8. Optional navigation or section-label recommendations if they make the positioning clearer.
